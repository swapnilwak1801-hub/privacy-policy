PeaceMode

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

PeaceMode is designed to work on your device. The app does not send your personal information or app settings to PeaceMode servers, and it does not include advertising or analytics services.

Information and app data

PeaceMode does not ask you to create an account and does not collect or transmit personal information, location, contacts, or device identifiers. To provide its features, the app saves limited functional data locally on your device, including your duration presets, whether a session is active, its scheduled end time, and sound levels needed to restore your settings after a session. This data is not sent to us.

Permissions and how they are used

  • Do Not Disturb / notification policy access: to apply the selected interruption mode during a PeaceMode session.
  • Audio settings: to silence supported sound streams for a session and restore saved sound levels when the session ends.
  • Display over other apps: to show the optional floating control widget.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to support the app's ongoing session service and its status notification.
  • Exact alarms: to schedule a session end time so PeaceMode can restore sound settings when the session finishes.

These permissions are used for PeaceMode features and do not give us remote access to your device. You can manage or revoke permissions in Android settings; some features may stop working if a required permission is revoked.

Storage, backup, and deletion

App settings are stored in private app storage on your device. Android device backup may include app data if backup is enabled in your device or account settings. Any such backup is managed by Android and your backup provider, not by PeaceMode. You can clear the app's data or uninstall the app to remove its locally stored data; backup copies, if any, are subject to your Android backup settings and provider.

Third-party services and sharing

PeaceMode does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. We do not sell, share, or disclose app data because the app does not send it to us. Android and your device manufacturer may process information as part of operating system features, permissions, notifications, or backup; their own privacy terms apply to those services.

Children's privacy

PeaceMode does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date above indicates when the current version took effect.
