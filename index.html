PeaceMode

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

PeaceMode is designed to help you stay focused by managing sound sessions on your device. We respect your privacy and use Google Firebase services solely for app stability monitoring and basic usage analytics.

Information and App Data

PeaceMode does not require account registration and does not collect or transmit personal identifying information such as your name, email address, contacts, or location. To deliver its focus features, the app stores functional data locally on your device, including your custom duration presets, active session state, end times, and saved sound levels needed to restore your ringer settings.

Permissions and How They Are Used

  • Do Not Disturb / Notification Policy Access: to apply and restore selected interruption modes during focus sessions.
  • Audio Settings: to silence supported sound streams during a session and restore saved volume levels when it completes.
  • Display Over Other Apps: to present the optional floating widget overlay on screen.
  • Notifications and Foreground Service: to maintain ongoing session timer accuracy and display live status.
  • Exact Alarms: to ensure background session timers finish precisely on time.

Analytics & Crash Reporting (Third-Party Services)

To maintain app reliability, monitor performance, and fix software crashes, PeaceMode integrates services provided by Google LLC:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: collects pseudonymous usage statistics (such as feature usage events, session frequency, and app interaction data) to help us understand and improve user experience.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: automatically collects crash reports, stack traces, and device diagnostic data (such as OS version and device hardware model) when an unexpected error occurs, allowing us to diagnose and resolve software bugs.

These services do not collect personal identifiers. For more details on how Google handles collected diagnostic data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and the Firebase Privacy and Security Documentation.

Storage, Backup, and Deletion

Functional app settings are stored locally in private storage on your device. You can clear local app data or uninstall PeaceMode at any time to remove all locally stored preferences.

Children's Privacy

PeaceMode does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically if app features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when the current version took effect.
